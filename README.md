# shooting-star
This is a pygame project I made for Snek project in Hack Club.
This is the first game I tried to code with any asset.
You are a shooting star in the vast space. Your goal is to avoid colliding into other objects and reach the end goal (the green star).
The planets pull you by gravity, so you have to be aware of their pull strength.
Big planets have their moons, you also have to avoid colliding into the orbits
Black Hole pulls you, White Hole pushes you, and you will be sent to white hole through entering the black hole.
You will also have to try to avoid the meteors from outer space.
